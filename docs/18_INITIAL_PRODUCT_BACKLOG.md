# 18 Initial Product Backlog

## Milestone 0 — Platform foundation
- Bootstrap monorepo and local environment
- Authentication/RBAC/audit foundation
- CI, testing and security scanning
- Object storage and document pipeline

## Milestone 1 — Trusted environmental data core
- Source registry and immutable snapshots
- Administrative areas and spatial boundaries
- Dataset validation and monthly synchronization
- Provenance, freshness and conflict management

## Milestone 2 — Local environmental database MVP
- Geography/topography/land-use sections
- Geology and geological sensitivity
- Soil properties and background concentration records
- Surface hydrology
- Groundwater and hydrogeology
- Ten-year population
- Ten-year climate/rainfall
- Pollution sites, sensitive areas and investigation history

## Milestone 3 — GIS and analytics
- Interactive map/layer catalog
- Spatial identify, buffer and overlay
- Chart/table services
- Map export with source/date/CRS

## Milestone 4 — Report generation
- Section editor and custom length control
- Evidence-grounded generation and citation engine
- Version comparison and locking
- DOCX/PDF/HTML/Markdown export
- Export manifest and reproducibility

## Milestone 5 — Project workspace
- Projects, parcels, contacts and timeline
- Field photos/GPS/notes/offline queue
- Samples, boreholes, wells and documents
- Tasks, approvals and version history

## Milestone 6 — Legal and professional planning
- Article 8/9 decision-support workflow
- Control plan builder
- Remediation plan builder
- Review and approval gates

## Milestone 7 — Knowledge and enterprise operation
- Searchable references and prior cases
- Organization templates
- Monitoring, backups, billing readiness and administration

Priority order is milestone sequence. No feature may bypass source provenance, freshness, audit or human-review requirements.
