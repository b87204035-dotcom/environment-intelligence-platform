# 06 Legal, Control and Remediation Modules

## 1. Legal corpus

The system must version and index:
- Soil and Groundwater Pollution Remediation Act and Enforcement Rules
- Article 8 announced business categories and definitions
- Article 9 related rules, notices and public interpretations
- Control plan writing guideline
- Remediation plan writing guideline
- Site improvement review/supervision guidance
- Related sampling, testing, verification and control-zone rules

The legal corpus must be refreshed on schedule and every assessment must preserve the exact law/version used.

## 2. Article 8/9 decision engine

The engine is rule-assisted, not free-form AI. Steps:
1. Identify event type and relevant date.
2. Determine land/business identity and historic operators.
3. Match business facts to the effective announced-business definition.
4. Evaluate statutory conditions.
5. Return likely applicability, non-applicability or insufficient information.
6. Provide missing facts, provisions, notices and public interpretations.
7. Require professional approval before export.

## 3. Control plan builder

Required section families:
- applicant, writer and executor
- site history and surrounding environment
- investigation basis, sampling and QA/QC
- pollutant distribution and conceptual site model
- control objectives and compliance criteria
- control methods and design assumptions
- soil handling and groundwater extraction controls
- secondary pollution prevention
- monitoring and performance evaluation
- health, safety and emergency response
- schedule, budget, organization and reporting
- attachments, maps, tables and references

Completeness checks must distinguish missing mandatory content from optional enhancement.

## 4. Remediation plan builder

Required section families:
- site and responsibility background
- investigation and risk basis
- remediation objectives and future land use
- technology screening and alternatives comparison
- pilot/bench test evidence
- engineering design and implementation
- chemical/biological reaction and injection details where applicable
- monitoring, contingency and alternative method triggers
- verification and completion criteria
- soil disposition, waste, emissions and secondary pollution
- green and sustainable remediation evaluation
- schedule, costs and institutional controls

## 5. Engineering guardrails

- Recommendations must list required site-specific inputs.
- Cost estimates are ranges with assumptions, location/year and exclusions.
- No automatic injection dose, well spacing or final remediation target without professional review.
- Generated text labels unsupported statements and data gaps.
- Exported drafts carry status: draft, reviewed or approved.
