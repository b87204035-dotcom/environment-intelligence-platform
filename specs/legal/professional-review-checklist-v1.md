# Legal and Professional Review Checklist v1

## Article 8/9 preliminary evaluation

- [ ] Correct law/rule title and effective version selected
- [ ] Transaction/action type confirmed
- [ ] Announced-business classification supported by official evidence
- [ ] Site identity, address, parcel and business entity reconciled
- [ ] Required investigation trigger and exemptions reviewed
- [ ] Missing facts explicitly listed
- [ ] Automated result labeled preliminary
- [ ] Reviewer name, qualification, date and decision recorded

## Environmental interpretation

- [ ] Geographic scale is appropriate for the conclusion
- [ ] Data periods and missing observations are disclosed
- [ ] Soil background values are not confused with standards
- [ ] Groundwater direction claims have sufficient head/elevation evidence
- [ ] Pollution overlap/proximity terminology is spatially correct
- [ ] Historical investigation statements are traceable to reports or official records
- [ ] No absence-of-data statement is converted into absence-of-impact

## Control/remediation plan

- [ ] Conceptual site model is evidence-based and internally consistent
- [ ] Contaminants, media, extent and receptors are supported
- [ ] Objectives and performance criteria are measurable
- [ ] Alternatives and selection basis are documented
- [ ] Design assumptions, quantities, schedule and cost basis are explicit
- [ ] Monitoring, contingency and verification are complete
- [ ] Required authority format and current guidance version checked
- [ ] Final professional approval and signature status recorded

## Publication

- [ ] All factual paragraphs have evidence links
- [ ] Tables, figures and maps have sources and dates
- [ ] APA references resolve to registered sources
- [ ] QA findings are closed or formally accepted
- [ ] Approved version checksum is stored
- [ ] Exported document matches the approved content snapshot
