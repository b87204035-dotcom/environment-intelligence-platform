# 16 Report Template Catalog

## Common document rules
All templates support editable title page, approval page, automatic table of contents, chapter numbering, list of figures/tables, citations, reference list, appendices, revision table and export manifest.

## Template A: Local Environmental Baseline Report
1. Purpose and scope
2. Study area and data period
3. Geographic location and administrative overview
4. Topography, area and land use
5. Regional geology and geological sensitivity
6. Soil classification, properties and background data
7. Surface water and drainage
8. Groundwater and hydrogeology
9. Ten-year population analysis
10. Ten-year climate and rainfall analysis
11. Pollution sites and regulated business context
12. Environmental sensitive areas
13. Soil and groundwater investigation history
14. Integrated interpretation and limitations
15. References
16. Source and update manifest

Default narrative target is 880 Chinese characters per major section, independently adjustable.

## Template B: Article 8/9 Decision-Support Report
- Transaction or business context
- Land, address and parcel identification
- Business/activity classification evidence
- Historical land use and operational evidence
- Pollution-site and surrounding context
- Applicable legal version and decision tree
- Preliminary conclusion, assumptions, missing evidence and required professional verification
- Attachments and references

## Template C: Phase I Environmental Site Assessment
- User/owner information
- Site reconnaissance
- Historical records
- Regulatory records
- Interviews
- Environmental setting
- Recognized environmental conditions framework configurable to the selected standard
- Data gaps, conclusions and references

## Template D: Control Plan
- Site and pollution status
- Conceptual site model
- Control objectives and regulatory basis
- Immediate and long-term control measures
- Engineering design basis
- Health/safety and environmental protection
- Monitoring, sampling, QA/QC and performance indicators
- Schedule, organization and budget
- Contingency and reporting
- References and appendices

## Template E: Remediation Plan
- Site characterization and remediation objectives
- Remedial alternatives screening
- Treatability/pilot testing
- Selected remedy and design
- Construction and operation
- Monitoring and verification
- Risk management, contingency, closure and post-remediation management
- Schedule, cost and responsibilities
- References and appendices

## Template F: Investigation and Monitoring Report
- Investigation objectives
- Sampling design
- Field methods
- Laboratory methods and QA/QC
- Results and spatial/temporal interpretation
- Conceptual site model update
- Conclusions and recommendations

## Publication controls
Legal, control and remediation documents cannot reach Approved status without a designated qualified reviewer. AI-generated content is visually labeled until reviewed.
